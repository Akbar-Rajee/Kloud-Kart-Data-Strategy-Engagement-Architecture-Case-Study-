# Document : Improvement Beyond the Brief

## Deletion-Request Response Playbook

### What It Is

A documented, step-by-step process for responding to a customer data deletion request under Ghana's Data Protection Act, written so any member of Nana's team can execute it without technical help.

### Why the Brief Does Not Cover It

The brief requires a customer data export register and compliance readiness as outcomes. It does not specify how a deletion request is actually handled once a complaint arrives. The register tells Kloud Kart where data lives. The playbook tells a non-technical team member what to do with that information within 90 days.

### What It Covers

1. How to receive and log a deletion request (email, form, or verbal)
2. How to search the export register for all copies of the requesting customer's data
3. Who approves the deletion at each location (owner field in the register)
4. How to confirm deletion at each location and record the confirmation
5. How to notify the customer that their request has been completed
6. What to file if the Data Protection Commission requests evidence of compliance

### What It Is Worth to Nana

A DPC complaint that Kloud Kart cannot respond to within 90 days can result in a formal investigation, public finding, and financial penalty under Act 843. The penalty ceiling is not the primary risk. The board finding out that Kloud Kart received a DPC complaint it could not handle, in the same year it told investors it had a data strategy, is. The playbook costs one day of the Data Engineer's time in month one to draft and costs nothing to maintain. It converts a governance gap into a closed item before the board asks about it.
