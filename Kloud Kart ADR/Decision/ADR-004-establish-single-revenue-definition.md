# ADR-004: Establish one company-wide revenue definition

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Decision owner:** Nana

## Context

Finance and Operations currently report different revenue values. This undermines confidence in management reporting and weakens decision-making.

The business requires one trusted version of the truth.

## Decision

One agreed company-wide revenue definition will be established and used by all reporting outputs.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative A: Department-owned revenue definitions

- **How it would work:** Each department maintains independent revenue calculations.
- **Why it is attractive:** Minimal change-management effort.
- **Why rejected:** Produces conflicting numbers and reduces trust in reporting.

### Alternative B: One shared revenue definition

- **How it would work:** Revenue is defined centrally and reused across reporting assets.
- **Why it is attractive:** Creates consistency and trust.
- **Why rejected:** Not rejected.

## Consequences

- **What this buys:** Consistent reporting and greater confidence in board decisions.
- **What this costs:** Stakeholder alignment effort and governance overhead.
- **What it costs the client if I am wrong:** Decisions may be based on an incorrect definition that affects planning and resource allocation.
- **What would make me revisit this:** A material change in the business model or revenue-recognition requirements.

## What this survives

- Department growth.
- New reports.
- Additional reporting consumers.
